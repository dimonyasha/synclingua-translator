# SyncLingua Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 6, 2026**

SyncLingua is built with your privacy as a priority. We do not collect telemetry, show ads, or share your data with third parties without your knowledge.

Starting from version 1.1.0, the project supports two translation modes. It is important to understand the difference:

### 1. Local Mode (Default)
*   **Audio:** Captured locally and never leaves your computer.
*   **Speech Recognition:** Performed locally (Whisper).
*   **Translation:** Performed locally (Ollama).
*   **Speech Synthesis:** Performed locally (Piper).
*   **Privacy:** 100%. Not a single byte of your data is sent to the internet.

### 2. Cloud Mode (Optional)
If you explicitly select cloud mode (e.g., GigaChat or OpenRouter) in the extension settings:
*   **Audio:** Still recognized **locally** on your PC.
*   **Text:** The recognized text is sent to the API server you specified for translation.
*   **Responsibility:** In this mode, text processing is governed by the privacy policy of your chosen provider (e.g., Sber).

### Key Security
All API keys are stored locally in your browser (Chrome Storage) and are sent only to the server you specified.

**The SyncLingua developer has no access to your keys or your traffic.**

### Website Analytics
To improve the performance of our official website (`synclingua.ru`), we use the anonymized statistics service **Yandex.Metrika**. 
This service collects technical information about visits (such as device type, browser, and entry pages), but **does not identify you as an individual**.
We use this data only to understand which sections of the website are most useful and to fix errors.

You can opt out of data collection by installing the official [Yandex.Metrika Blocker](https://yandex.ru/legal/metrica_termsofuse/) browser extension.
